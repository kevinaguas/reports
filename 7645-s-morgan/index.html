What is a CMA?

A comparative market analysis estimates what a property is worth by comparing it with similar properties that recently sold nearby. For 2 to 4 unit buildings, the best comparables match on unit count, construction, garden-level setup, parking and condition.

How this one was prepared

    This analysis is a broker opinion based on market data, not an appraisal. Information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed. Listing photos and remarks are courtesy of the listing brokers via the MRED MLS.
